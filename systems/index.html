Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a technical background to work with you?

No. You bring knowledge of your business, and we handle the technical side and explain decisions in plain language.

How is pricing set?

Every project is scoped individually. After a first conversation, you receive a proposal with a clear scope, timeline, and quote before any work begins.

Who owns what you build?

You do. The finished system and its files belong to your business.

Can you work with tools we already use?

Usually, yes. Many projects connect to or build on the software you already have, so your team doesn't have to start over.
