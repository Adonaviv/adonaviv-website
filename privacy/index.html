Privacy policy

What we collect through this website, why we collect it, and how to ask us to change or delete it. Effective November 16, 2026.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us through a form on this site:

  • Contact and team training forms: your name, email, organization, team size, preferred format or timing, and your message.
  • AI Readiness Check: your name, email, organization, team size, your answers, and your score. If you finish the check without leaving contact details, we receive your answers and score with no name or email attached. If you start the check from an event link, we receive the details you enter before starting, even if you don't finish.
  • Digital business card: the contact details you choose to share.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and send what you asked for, such as your readiness results or a proposal
  • To plan and deliver training or projects you request
  • To follow up about your inquiry. We won't add you to a newsletter unless you ask to join one.

Who processes it

This site is hosted by Netlify, which receives and stores form submissions for us. Submissions are forwarded to our business email, provided by Google Workspace. These providers process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms. We may also use privacy friendly analytics that count visits without cookies or personal profiles.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as we need them to respond and to manage any work that follows. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete the information you sent us, or to stop hearing from us, email hello@adonaviv.com. We'll respond within one business day and complete the request promptly.

Children

This site is meant for organizations and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
